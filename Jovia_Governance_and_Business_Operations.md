- **Sponsored Courses** — Only from accredited providers with completion-to-income data

- **Sponsored Banking** — Freelancer-friendly financial services (multi-currency, low-fee)
- **Sponsored AI Services** — Productivity-enhancing AI tools (with performance guarantees)
- **Sponsored Hardware** — Equipment that increases output quality (monitors, microphones, etc.)
- **Sponsored Internet Providers** — Reliable connectivity for remote work
