# AI Continuous Opportunity Discovery Engine

One of Jovia's strongest competitive advantages must be the **Continuous
AI Opportunity Discovery Engine**.

Unlike traditional freelance platforms that require users to manually
search for projects, **Jovia continuously searches for opportunities on
behalf of every freelancer**.

## Continuous Opportunity Monitoring

-   Discover new opportunities automatically.
-   Analyze them using AI.
-   Rank them by priority.
-   Recommend the best opportunities.

## Dynamic Search Frequency

-   New users: every **5--10 minutes**
-   Highly active freelancers: every **3--5 minutes**
-   Pro subscribers: **Near Real-Time**
-   Low-activity users: every **15--30 minutes**
-   Enterprise customers: configurable intervals.

Frequency adapts to: - User activity - Subscription tier - Current
workload - Market activity - Number of matching opportunities

## Intelligent Filtering

AI evaluates: - Skills matching - Experience matching - Budget - Client
quality - Competition - Winning probability - Expected ROI - Career
growth

## Instant Notifications

Every high-value opportunity should include: - Opportunity Score - Match
explanation - Recommended pricing - Winning probability - AI-generated
proposal - One-click Apply

## Ultimate Vision

**Jovia never sleeps.**

Instead of freelancers searching for projects, **Jovia searches for
projects on behalf of freelancers 24/7**.
